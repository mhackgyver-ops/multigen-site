Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Multi_gen ("the App") is a personal tool used only by its author with their own TikTok account.

Data accessed

  • Basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) returned by TikTok Login Kit (user.info.basic).
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to upload videos as drafts (video.upload).

How data is used and stored

This data is used only to upload the author's own videos to the author's own TikTok account. It is stored locally on the author's computer, is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the App is connected. Deleting the local token file, or revoking access in TikTok settings (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions), removes all access immediately.

Children

The App is not directed at children and does not collect data from anyone other than its author.
